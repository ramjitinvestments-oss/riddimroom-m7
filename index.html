2 Transcribe on this device

An open Whisper speech model runs in your browser and writes word-timed captions. The model downloads once (about 40 MB for Fast, 145 MB for Accurate) and is then cached on your device.

Getting ready…

If this browser can’t run the speech model, you never hit a dead end: type captions by hand below, or import an SRT/VTT file, and everything else — styles and downloads — still works.

3 Edit captions

Tap a caption — or tap a word on the video — to fix it. Times are in seconds; the text is exactly what appears in your download. Your captions are saved on this device as you work, so a refresh won’t lose them.

Word emoji

Words like fire, money, love, beach and rum get their emoji right after them in the caption — in the preview and the download only, never in your caption text or the SRT/VTT files. A little adds at most one per caption; Plenty adds every match.
